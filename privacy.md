# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 13, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
SNS Automation ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a personal social media content management and publishing tool operated under the name "SNS Automation".

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when the Service connects to TikTok and other supported social media platforms.

## 2. Information We Collect
When an account holder authorizes the Service through TikTok Login Kit, the Service may access:

- Basic account information, such as a user identifier, display name, and profile picture, where permitted.
- Authorization tokens necessary to access the authorized account.
- Video files and related content selected for uploading.

The Service only requests information needed for its functionality.

## 3. How We Use Information
Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and upload content, maintain integrations, and troubleshoot technical issues.

We do not sell personal information or use TikTok account information for unrelated advertising.

## 4. Information Sharing
Information may be transmitted to TikTok and other connected platforms as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

We do not share personal information with unrelated third parties for marketing purposes.

## 5. Data Storage and Security
Authentication credentials and related information are intended to be stored securely and accessed only as needed to operate the Service.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information against unauthorized access.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the Service or meet applicable requirements.

Account holders may request deletion of information held by the Service by contacting the operator.

## 7. Third-Party Services
Use of TikTok and other connected platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Service or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page.

## 9. Contact
For privacy-related inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact the operator through the contact information provided with the Service.
